Services

Residential & Commercial Paving Services

From residential driveways and patios to commercial developments, estate roads and specialised paving projects, we deliver durable, professionally installed paving solutions tailored to every client.

Driveways

Paving engineered to handle daily vehicle weight without shifting or cracking, in a pattern that suits the home.

Patios

Outdoor entertainment areas laid for comfort underfoot and long-term durability.

Estate Roads

Large-scale access roads and common areas project-managed for estates and developers, on schedule and on budget.

Pool Areas

Slip-resistant, chemical-tolerant surfaces designed to stay level and secure around water for years to come.

Kerbing

Clean, precise edging that defines driveways, gardens and roadways while protecting surrounding surfaces.

Repairs & Maintenance

Restoring sunken, cracked or uneven paving to its original condition, extending the life of existing work.

Why Choose Us

Built on Quality Since 1982

For more than four decades, Paving Unlimited has been delivering high-quality paving solutions across Johannesburg. Our reputation has been built on reliable service, skilled workmanship and lasting results for hundreds of satisfied residential and commercial clients.

Around Since 1982

We've paved through changing trends, building codes and three decades of Johannesburg weather. That kind of experience isn't something you can fake.

You Deal With the Same Person

Whoever quotes your job is the same person who checks the falls and finishes before we call it done — not a rotating cast of site managers.
